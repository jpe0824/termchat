# termchat

A fast, reliable, chat-only TUI that runs in any terminal.

## Goals

- **Chat only.** One job, done well — no feature sprawl.
- **Fast and reliable.** Instant startup, no dropped messages.
- **Keyboard and mouse navigation.** Fully usable with either.
- **Open source, local storage.** Your history stays on your machine.
- **Works in any terminal.** No dependence on a specific emulator or font.
- **AI CLI integration.** Hook into third-party AI CLIs such as herdr.
- **Agent CLI.** A command-line interface that lets agents read and send messages without driving the TUI.
- **Chat platform bridges (later).** Possible connectors for Slack, Teams, Discord, and similar.
- **Files and attachments as downloads.** Opt-in per user; nothing is fetched unless you ask for it.

## Non-goals

- **Not a replacement for other chat clients' full UIs.** Bridges cover chat, not every feature of the platform.
- **Not an AI chat interface.** AI conversations happen through CLI integrations, not a built-in assistant.
- **No calling or screen sharing.**
